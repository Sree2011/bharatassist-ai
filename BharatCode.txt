Project Name: BharatAssist AI

Hackathon: BharatCode Build League

Description:
BharatAssist AI is a multi-agent citizen welfare navigator that helps Indian
citizens discover government schemes, verify eligibility, understand required
documents, and generate personalized action plans.

Built With:
- Python
- Streamlit
- Google Gemini (gemini-2.0-flash)
- Agentic AI

Problem Solved:
Many citizens are unaware of welfare schemes and struggle to understand
eligibility requirements and application processes. BharatAssist AI matches
citizens against a curated scheme database using explicit, auditable rules,
explains why they qualify, and generates a personalized application roadmap
in six Indian languages.
